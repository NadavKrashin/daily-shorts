# voice.md (Why in Clay)

The voice profile the yt-* skills read. Scheduled runs copy it to `~/.claude/youtube/voice.md`
before using those skills.

## Who I am talking to

One curious person, 14 to 40, scrolling Shorts on a phone with the sound on, who has noticed
something strange in everyday life (a jam with no crash, a curved flight path) and never got a
straight answer.

## How I actually talk

Bram, the narrator (Kevin voiced episode 1): clear, warm and unhurried; every word lands. Short sentences that turn on a
reveal. Example from episode 1:

> This enormous traffic jam has no cause. Drive to its very front and you'll find no accident, no
> roadwork, just cars accelerating away.

> Here's the surprise: the wave travels backwards, and on real highways jams drift upstream at
> roughly fifteen to twenty kilometres an hour.

## Words I never use

"did you know", "hey guys", "welcome back", "in this video", "mind-blowing", "insane", "you won't
believe", "let's dive in", "game-changer". No subscribe pitch in the first 50 seconds.

## Words I do use

"Here's the surprise", "the twist", "roughly", "it turns out", "which means". Numbers spoken as
words.

## What I will not claim

Any number, study or quote I cannot source this run. No "scientists say" without naming who. No
medical, legal or financial advice. No claims that a clay model is real footage.

## My format

60-second vertical Shorts, six 10-second scenes, no host on camera, handmade claymation, captions
on. The payoff always answers the hook, and the last line hands the idea back to the viewer.

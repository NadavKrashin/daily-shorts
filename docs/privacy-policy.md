# Why in Clay Publisher: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 16 October 2026*

**Why in Clay** is an independent educational YouTube channel that publishes short animated
explainers. **Why in Clay Publisher** is a private, internal tool used only by the channel's owner.
It uploads the channel's own videos to the channel's own YouTube account and reads that channel's
own statistics. It has no outside users. Nobody else can sign in to it.

## YouTube API Services

Why in Clay Publisher uses **YouTube API Services**. By using this tool you agree to be bound by the
YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms. Google's handling of data is described in
the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

## What data the tool accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool receives permission to:

- **Upload videos** to the Why in Clay channel (`youtube.upload`).
- **Read the channel's own videos and basic channel details** (`youtube.readonly`).
- **Read the channel's own YouTube Analytics** (`yt-analytics.readonly`), such as views, watch time
  and audience retention for our own videos.

The tool does not access any other channel's data, viewers' personal information, comments from
other users' channels, or any data unrelated to our own channel.

## How the data is used

- Uploaded videos, titles, descriptions and disclosures are sent to YouTube to publish them on our
  channel.
- Our own channel statistics are used only to understand which of our videos are useful to viewers,
  so we can decide what to make next.

## Storage and sharing

- The Google sign-in credentials (OAuth tokens) and the statistics are stored only on the channel
  owner's own computer, in a private database that is not reachable from the internet.
- Statistics are kept while the channel uses the tool. If the owner revokes the tool's access, the
  stored YouTube statistics are deleted within 30 days.
- We **do not sell, share, rent or disclose** YouTube data to anyone, and we do not use it for
  advertising.

## Revoking access

Access granted to Why in Clay Publisher can be revoked at any time through the Google security
settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
